# Evaluation assets and provenance

[official_data_manifest.json](official_data_manifest.json) inventories local
sources and pinned revisions. The retained [RA-4M subset](RA4M_50.md) has 50
original images and complete copied per-image positive GT. The source snapshot
was removed after extraction; old smoke files and full-snapshot plots are no
longer retained. The [current gallery](gallery/index.html) shows every image
and every model/backend/storage configuration.

Public PSG / IndoorVG / VG150 labelled subsets and labelled SpatialSense
valid/test cells are under `test_data/metric_subsets/`, with their own image
hashes, splits and category metadata. Their bounded evaluator regressions are
[reported independently in the complete report](summary/README.md), including
SpatialSense. They cannot replace the
published protocol packs or establish broad-dataset deployment performance.

The named [OV-SGG-Bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/maelic/OV-SGG-Bench)
pack is referenced by [upstream SPEC](../../benchmark/SPEC.md) and
[training docs](../../docs/training.md). A fresh 2026-10-09 `dataset_info` check
returned HTTP 401 anonymously and HTTP 404 with the locally cached identity;
The protocol-access evidence is included in the [complete report](summary/README.md)
and records status codes without credentials. No exact pack was downloaded. These checks do not establish
whether another identity could access it.

| Unavailable protocol family | Required assets |
|---|---|
| Soft open vocabulary | labelled synonym/inverse pairs, ontology and text-space matcher calibration |
| Haystack precision | adjudicated positive/negative judgments, category coverage and fixed split |
| SGDet | fixed detector predictions, operating point and pair-recall ceiling |
| Judge / HICO / OVS | held-out pairings, blinded judge controls and six-axis normalizers |
| ECE / Brier | label-complete held-out deployment calibration distribution |

Unannotated RA-4M relations are not negative labels. Free text generated by the
model is not a synonym calibration corpus. ECE/Brier on federated negative
cells would change the deployment protocol. These five families retain
`missing_asset` in the [published acceptance evidence](gallery/evidence/acceptance_status.json).
See [metric definitions and scope](METRIC_COVERAGE.md).

```sh
python3 cpp_ggml/scripts/download_test_data.py --dataset maelic/OV-SGG-Bench --dest cpp_ggml/test_data/official --allow-large
```

Input-parity, raw-logit comparison and complete end-to-end timing use retained
assets and run independently of the missing protocol pack.
